**As a** procmgr author, **I want to** have Space and Thread capabilities, full process teardown and timers, **so that** processes can be created, signalled and destroyed without leaks.

<objective>
Process-level teardown (KERN-08, first half; OD-04's process level). Covered here:
- the last-thread exit and `Space::kill` free a process's memory and return its charged pages;
- they wake its futex waiters;
- they answer callers parked on its dead servers.

02-07 does agent-level teardown (CSpace drain, carve return), staging-page charges, armed-timer cleanup and the full counter set.

This plan is a DRAFT written against the post-01-07 tree (OD-10), on top of 02-04 and 02-05. Re-check after 01-07 lands:
- Task 1: whether `load_image` still allocates the stack inside the image space (the `pages` count includes it), and the lib.rs boot checks that call `start` (lib.rs:50, :106; `user::run` no longer exists). They adopt their spaces, so they keep working.
- Task 2: the 01-07 `futex_waiter` and `spinner` bins used by the kill tests.

It stops first at a blocking checkpoint on OD-04, OD-07 and OD-08. They are assumed decisions, and OD-04 is costly: procmgr's lifecycle builds on it.

Purpose: success criterion 3. Without teardown, every exited process keeps its frames and its owner's budget, and every caller of a dead server waits forever.

- `Space` ownership modes, the charge ledger, `teardown`, `Space::kill` and `Space::from_image`;
- `Agent::uncharge_pages` and the `Payer` trait;
- `Thread::{kill, retire}` and `Exit::Killed`;

  <behavior>
    - `teardown_loop_same_agent_10000x`:
      - Setup:
        - agent A, memory limit 64 pages;
        - holder thread H of A;
        - parent image P = `fault_sibling`, with its test data page mapped writable and copy-on-write after the clone.
      - Each of 10,000 iterations:
        1. Build child space C = `Space::new(Arc::new(cow_clone(P)), A)`, insert a Space capability for C (CREATE) into A's CSpace, and keep a `Weak<Space>` to C.
        2. Run `thread_create(H, slot, arg 1)` and `join_thread`.
        3. Remove both capabilities.
      - Every iteration exits `Exit::Code(8)`, so the child broke copy-on-write once.
      - After each iteration, the weak reference stops upgrading within 1,000 yields.
      - After the loop:
        - `A.mem_used_pages()` equals its value before the loop;
        - `A.cspace.lock().room()` equals its value before the loop.
    - `teardown_charges_image_and_stack_pages`:
      - `Space::from_image(load_image(fault_sibling), A)` raises `A.mem_used_pages()` by exactly `img.pages`;
      - the thread's exit returns it to the earlier value;
      - with a budget smaller than `img.pages`, `from_image` is `Err(AgentError::Budget)` and charges nothing.
    - `teardown_unmaps_owned_space_and_leaves_a_tombstone`:
      - After the last exit of an owned space, querying the image base in its `vm()` finds nothing mapped;
      - `is_dying()` is true;
      - `thread_create` into it is `SpaceError::Dead`;
      - its notify capability is gone.
    - `teardown_keeps_an_adopted_space_mapped`:
      - `start` on a caller-held image leaves the caller's mappings readable after exit;
      - the copy-on-write pages charged during the run are still uncharged.
  </behavior>

## Trust Boundaries

| Boundary | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| process → kernel | A process exits or is killed at any point, including inside kernel waits |
| creator → dying process | A holder may race `thread_create` against kill or the last exit |
