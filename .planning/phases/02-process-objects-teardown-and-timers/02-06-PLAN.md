**As a** procmgr author, **I want to** have Space and Thread capabilities, full process teardown and timers, **so that** processes can be created, signalled and destroyed without leaks.

<objective>
Process-level teardown (KERN-08, first half; D-01's process level, OD-04). Covered here:
- the last-thread exit and `Space::kill` free a process's memory and return its charged pages;
- they wake its futex waiters;
- they answer callers parked on its dead servers;
- `cow_clone` becomes fallible and unwinds a partial clone (D-05, D-07, RR-01 and RR-03 from the Phase 1 verification). It charges the child's page-table node frames through a `&dyn Payer`, and `handle_cow_fault` loses its two panicking unwraps.

02-07 does agent-level teardown (D-08 retire, CSpace drain, carve return), staging-page charges, the futex errno mapping and the node-charge ledger check (D-05's 02-07 half), armed-timer cleanup, the full counter set and the D-03 free-frame gate.
